## Reproduction

Tester's stack, demo seed. Any block that sits on a job (every seeded block does; the tester used a
job and its block on Cell 1 today from 3 pm to 5 pm, Sam Patel). Sign in as **Ana**, open the bar
and type **"extend Sam Patel by 30 minutes"** (or "extend everyone on Cell 1 by 30 minutes" --
DEF-0046's sentence, which no longer crashes). The board opens its own pop-up, "Continue? This takes
Sam Patel off the Housing A 15:00-17:00 run and makes them a standalone assignment." Three runs:
close the page with the pop-up standing; press **Cancel**; press **Continue**. After each, read the
bar's thread and the last lines of `data/voice/trace/bar.jsonl`.

No pin: the path runs through the board's confirm pop-up and the bar's render harness is not
exported.

## Expected

R-434 (a standard): "Anything the bar hears, asks, offers, writes or refuses is a trace entry and a
turn in the thread." R-431: every readout the bar prints as done is what was written. R-459: every
sentence the bar says is one a supervisor would say, never an internal word.

## Actual

```
SAY  extend Sam Patel by 30 minutes
Sam Patel's block on Cell 1 now ends 5:30 pm; it was 5 pm.
Waiting: a decision about the re-time (attachment or keep/scale)
POP-UP: Continue? This takes Sam Patel off the Housing A 15:00–17:00 run and makes them a standalone assignment. Continue? Cancel Continue
```

After **Continue** (the write happens; the thread and the trace do not say so):

```
BAR after Continue: Sam Patel's block on Cell 1 now ends 5:30 pm; it was 5 pm.
TRACE: NO ENTRY
block | Sam Patel | Wed 09-30 15:00 | Wed 09-30 17:30 | direct
```

After **Cancel** (nothing is written; the thread still reads as if it were):

```
BAR after Cancel: Sam Patel's block on Cell 1 now ends 5:30 pm; it was 5 pm.
TRACE after Cancel: NO ENTRY
```

With the page closed while the pop-up stands: `trace entries for this turn: []`. Zero page errors in
all three. For contrast, in the same session a question left open ("clear Cell 9 today") was in the
trace with `answered: null` the moment it was asked -- DEF-0049's fix holds for questions; this path
is not a question.

"Waiting: a decision about the re-time (attachment or keep/scale)" is shown to the person in the
thread; "re-time", "attachment" and "keep/scale" are the code's words.

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** `useDragGesture.ts` ~l.1979 answers the bar with
`waitingFor: "a decision about the re-time (attachment or keep/scale)"` when a re-time needs the
confirm pop-up (the string dates from 18 Sept, 6b8a84e, before R-459's wording pass, which did not
reach it). The bar shows that status and then seems to have no one to hear the pop-up's answer:
neither `confirmYes` nor `confirmNo` reports back, so the entry opened for the sentence is never
posted (DEF-0049's post-at-the-ask covers questions and lots, not a waiting status) and never
finished. The pop-up's own answer could settle the turn the way the create pop-up's reporter does
(`report({kind: "written"})` / `refused` / a cancel), and the waiting line could say what the person
is being asked: "The board is asking whether to take Sam Patel off the Housing A job."

## Developer note -- 30 Sept, session 195

Reproduced in the browser as Ana first (the thread's "now ends 2:30 pm" with the pop-up standing,
the trace empty, Cancel leaving the readout). Every pop-up the bar can hand a sentence to now
reports its answer through the create pop-up's own reporter, once: the Continue? question and its
keep-or-scale form, the crew outside a job's new hours, the split, and the three create forms. While
one stands the thread says one plain sentence built from the board's facts ("The board is asking
whether to take Sam Patel off the Housing A job. Answer it on the board."), the entry is posted at
the ask with no answer, and no done-form readout is printed before it is done. Continue settles
"Written: ...", Cancel or closing "Cancelled. Nothing changed.", a refusal after Continue "Not done:
<reason>", each with its trace outcome. A pop-up opened by hand that replaces the question answers
the sentence cancelled (found by the review). A lot step that would need such a question is refused
in words and carries on to Not tried. Seen again in the browser after the fix; the typed walk holds
Cancel, Continue, a typed "no" and the hand-opened pop-up as browser cases. Queued as F-240: a typed
"yes" does nothing, a reload leaves the waiting line, and a two-step re-time keeps the first
question's sentence.
