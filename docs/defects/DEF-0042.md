## Reproduction

On a freshly reset database with the current seed (the tester's: `node scripts/tester-stack.mjs up`,
which resets and seeds; the developer's: `npm run db:reset`, never while the app is in use), run the
walk: `node scripts/tester-run.mjs --e2e`, or
`npx playwright test e2e/typedWalk.spec.ts --project=chromium --workers=1` with the stack's env.

## Expected

R-433: the walk "runs green twice as an end-to-end spec over the same data file" before any
hand-over; R-459 lists `e2e/typedWalk.spec.ts` as its e2e proof ("both sentence lists green twice
against the new wording"). The walk's first sentence, "clear Cell 4 <walk day>", is written to find
the cell already empty (`expected: /^Cell 4 has nobody on it .+\.$/`), on the stated ground
(`e2e/walk/time.ts`, `walkDayInZone`) that "the demo seed, anchored on the current week, never
writes here".

## Actual

From `test-results/tester-run-2026-09-28-1429.md` (tester's stack, reset and seeded this session):

```
SAY:     clear Cell 4 2026-10-05
BAR:     Ready to do 2 things: 1. Priya Shah's block on Cell 4 now ends midnight; it was 6 am.; 2. The Housing A job is off Cell 4 Mon Oct 5; that was 10 pm to 6 am, for 1 people. Say yes to do them, or no.
!! UNPREDICTED: the bar answered this sentence with something else
  expected: /^Cell 4 has nobody on it .+\.$/
...
  3) [chromium] › e2e\typedWalk.spec.ts:511:3 › the typed command bar walks the real board (S61-c) › every sentence in the catalogue runs on the real bar, its writes prove out in the database, and the trace records it
    Error: the two areas' listings together should name every block and every job the walk left on the walk day
    Expected: 10
    Received: 14
```

The seed (`dev_demo.sql` ~l.472) writes Monday to Sunday of the current week; since R-452 (1d8d82c,
23 Sept) each block sits in its operator's own band, and Shift 3 is 22:00-06:00, so Sunday's night
blocks run to 06:00 on the next Monday -- the walk's day, on every run in the same week as the
reset. Session 190 records that the developer's database was not reset after R-452 ("the local
database not reset --- the maintainer's app was on it"), so session 191's two green runs were on the
old seed. The same run's other two e2e failures (`weekTemplates` afterAll timeout, `touch` T3 chip
not found) are not claimed here; they were not re-run in isolation this session.

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** Either the walk's day moves clear of the seed's spill (the Tuesday of
next week, or a Monday two weeks out), or its cleanup window starts at the previous day's 18:00 so
the spill is cleared first. The comment in `walkDayInZone` is the doc-drift half. Note the first
sentence also exposed DEF-0040 (the same clear deletes the Sunday part it says it keeps) and the
"1 people" wording in DEF-0043 -- the walk, run on the seed it claims, would have found both.
