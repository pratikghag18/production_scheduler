## Reproduction

Tester's stack, fresh demo seed (Priya Shah on Cell 4, Line 2, every day 6 am to 2 pm). Sign in as
**Ana**, open the bar:

1. **"put Priya Shah on Housing A at Cell 2 today from 10 am to noon"** (Cell 2 has no job at that
   hour).
2. **"put Priya Shah on Housing A at Cell 1 today from 10 am to noon"** (Cell 1 has a job 6 am to 2
   pm), then press either answer.

Read the thread's bubbles and the trace entry.

## Expected

R-465: "... and the bar refuses before the yes, never after it (R-431)." R-431 (a standard): "Every
button the bar offers and every readout it prints as done is decided by the check the server runs:
certificates, the area rule, capacity, absence." The capacity answer is known before anything is
said: the probe the bar asks after the fact answers it.

## Actual

Sentence 1, the thread's own bubbles for the one turn:

```
TURN 0: ["[you] put Priya Shah on Housing A at Cell 2 today from 10 am to noon",
         "[board] Priya Shah is on Cell 2 Wed Sep 30 from 10 am to noon, making Housing A.",
         "[board] Not done: Priya Shah is already on Cell 4 in Line 2 today from 6 am to 2 pm."]
trace: "asked":"Priya Shah is on Cell 2 Wed Sep 30 from 10 am to noon, making Housing A.", "ran":[], "outcome":"refused: Priya Shah is already on Cell 4 in Line 2 today from 6 am to 2 pm."
```

The screenshot shows the two board bubbles one under the other: a statement that Priya is on Cell 2,
then that she is not.

Sentence 2:

```
A Housing A job is already booked on Cell 1, 6 am to 2 pm. Join it, or make a separate block?
Join the Housing A job, 6 am to 2 pm
Separate block
PRESS Join
Not done: Priya Shah is already on Cell 4 in Line 2 today from 6 am to 2 pm.
```

Both buttons lead to the same refusal. Nothing was written in either case (Priya has one block that
day, read back), so this is about what the person is told, not about the data. The server half of
DEF-0053 holds: the live script exits 0 and the write is refused.

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** The capacity probe is asked after the write is refused (the developer's
note on DEF-0053: "after a capacity refusal ... the probe is asked again and the same sentence
replaces the numbers"), so the ordinary single-sentence path prints its readout, and the join
question is asked, before anyone knows. Asking `capacity_probe` for the person and the span at
resolve time, as the absence and certificate checks are, would let the bar answer the one sentence
"Priya Shah is already on Cell 4 in Line 2 today from 6 am to 2 pm." and nothing else, with no join
question in front of it.

## Developer note -- 30 Sept, session 196

Agreed, and fixed as one piece with F-239 (the live line said as done before the server answered),
which was the same cause. The bar now asks capacity_probe before it prints a readout or a question:
a single step, a question whose every answer is a placement (the join question, which shift, block
exists, the reason questions), and every step of a lot before the listing. Both sentences of the
reproduction now answer with one line, "Not done: Priya Shah is already on Cell 4 in Line 2 today
from 6 am to 2 pm.", no readout, no buttons, trace outcome refused; seen in the browser as Ana
before and after, and held by two cases in the typed walk (DEF-0060 and F-239). A lot with a busy
step names it ("Not doing ... : she is already on ...") and lists the rest. While a write is in
flight the live line reads "Working…" and the trace entry is posted with outcome "writing".
