## Reproduction

Tester's stack, demo seed (Priya Shah is on Cell 4, Line 2, today 6 am to 2 pm; Ana supervises Line 1 only). This is
the single-sentence door of DEF-0065, which the developer recorded as F-254 "from the code, not run"; this is it run.

1. `docker exec supabase_db_production_scheduler_tester psql -U postgres -c "insert into public.profile_grants(profile_id,node_id,org_id,role) select 'a0000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000002', id, org_id, 'viewer' from public.nodes where name='Cell 4'"`
2. Sign in as ana@example.test, open "Tell the board", type: `put Priya Shah on Common Fastener at Cell 1 today from 8 am to 10 am`.
3. The board's "Split coverage -- Priya Shah" pop-up opens. Press "Split evenly", then "Confirm".
4. Remove the grant: `delete from public.profile_grants where profile_id='a0000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000002' and role='viewer'`.

## Expected

R-431: "Every button the bar offers ... is decided by the check the server runs ... A refusal that arrives after the yes
is a defect." Ana cannot change Cell 4, so a split that rewrites Priya's Cell 4 block is something the server refuses;
the lot refuses that step up front since 0086 and the single sentence should do the same (or not offer Split).

## Actual

```
put Priya Shah on Common Fastener at Cell 1 today from 8 am to 10 am
Priya Shah is already booked then. The board is asking how to split the time. Answer it on the board.
(pop-up: Cell 4 100 / Cell 1 incoming 100, "Peak load: 200% (cap 100%)", buttons Cancel, Split evenly, Confirm)
... Confirm ...
Not done: You cannot change that block from here.
```

Nothing is written; the sentence is plain and carries no id. The damage is the offer and a refusal after the click.
The same pop-up is the one a drag or a create onto an overlapping block opens.

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** `openSplitPopover` (useDragGesture.ts) builds its rows from every overlapping probe row
without the `editable` flag that 0086 now returns; the fix is to read `editable` there (refuse before the pop-up, in
the same words as the lot). What a single sentence should say is the maintainer's call (F-254).

## Maintainer's decision -- 2 Oct, session 203t conversation

Of two candidates drawn side by side (A: refuse straight away in the lot's words, no pop-up; B: open the pop-up
without Split, with only Leave it), the maintainer chose A: "I agree with you." It is R-470 in the plan, and it
covers the board's drag and create door as well as the single sentence, since both open the same pop-up. Where Ana
can change every overlapping block, the pop-up still opens.
