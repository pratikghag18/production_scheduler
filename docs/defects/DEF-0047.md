## Reproduction

Tester's stack, demo seed (Maria Lopez on Line 1 Cell 2, Shift 3, 22:00-06:00 every night). Sign in
as **Ana** (Line 1 supervisor), open the bar, type **"clear Line 1 for the rest of the week"**.

Pin: `npx vitest run src/test/defects/DEF-0047.test.ts` (a named person's week clear over a night
block, on the REAL board axis -- `buildDayAxis`/`wallOf` -- in the week of the 8 March 2026 change).

## Expected

F-224 (session 191): a clear over a week is "a lot of one removal per day from today on". R-407: a
clear expands to one command per block. One block is one removal. R-459: the bar's answer is a
sentence a supervisor would say; the person said one sentence, not "two lines".

## Actual

As Ana (Playwright, tester's dev server):

```
SAY  clear Line 1 for the rest of the week
BAR  Moved the board to this week. Those two lines (2 and 5) are about the same block; say them one at a time.
```

The trace records only "Moved the board to this week." with no outcome (see DEF-0049). The pin:

```
× 'clear Priya Shah every day this week' removes her one Sat/Sun night block exactly ONCE, not once per day it overlaps
AssertionError: expected [ 'priyaNight', 'priyaNight' ] to deeply equal [ 'priyaNight' ]
```

Found by a tester lane on the resolver, re-run red by the tester, and seen live as Ana on the seed.

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** `expandRepeatUnassign` (resolve.ts ~5168-5206) loops the week's days
and, for each day whose window overlaps a block, pushes a removal for that assignment -- a block
across midnight overlaps two days and is queued twice. The bar's own duplicate guard
(`findDuplicateBlockPair`, CommandBar.tsx ~2061) then refuses the whole lot, which is why nothing
wrong is written but nothing can be done. De-duplicating by assignment id in the resolver would
leave the guard for genuinely repeated sentences. The words "two lines ... say them one at a time"
were written for a typed list of commands and read wrongly for one sentence.
