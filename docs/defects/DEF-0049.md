## Reproduction

Tester's dev server (`data/voice/trace/bar.jsonl` in the tester's worktree). In fresh pages, as Ana
or the admin, type one sentence each and close the page without answering: "Sam Patel is off
tomorrow", "John Kim is away tomorrow", "put Tom Baker on Cell 1 tomorrow from 8 am to noon", and
the garbled sentence of DEF-0046 with the model on. Then "extend everyone on Cell 1 by 30 minutes"
(DEF-0046's crash). Then "clear Line 1 for the rest of the week" as Ana (a move, then a refusal).
Read the trace file's entries for the afternoon.

## Expected

R-434: "Anything the bar hears, asks, offers, writes or refuses is a trace entry." R-455: "the trace
entry stays open across the move and the rerun's outcome closes it."

## Actual

Every trace entry from 15:20 on (tester's worktree), all of them:

```
15:25:25 | could you put Sam Patel on Housing A at Cell 1 tomorrow between 9 and 11 | answered: auto | outcome: written
15:32:48 | clear Cell 2 tomorrow | answered: no | outcome: cancelled
15:32:53 | John Kim is off tomorrow | asked: John Kim has no block Tue Sep 29. | answered: null | outcome:
15:32:57 | Maria Lopez is off tomorrow | asked: ... Remove which? | answered: null | outcome:
15:33:00 | clear Cell 3 today | asked: No cell called "Cell 3" on this board. | answered: null | outcome:
15:33:02 | clear Line 1 for the rest of the week | asked: Moved the board to this week. | answered: auto | outcome:
15:34:31 | clear Cell 1 for the rest of the week | answered: no | outcome: cancelled
15:36:12 | clear Cell 2 tomorrow | answered: Do all 4 | outcome: Done, 4 things.
```

Missing entirely: the four garbled-sentence turns, both "extend everyone" crashes, and the
single-sentence pages whose question was left open ("John Kim is away tomorrow", "Sam Patel is off
tomorrow", the second "clear Cell 3 today", "put Tom Baker ..."). The entries that exist for open
questions exist only because a later sentence in the same page closed them. The week clear's entry
ends at the move: the refusal the person read ("Those two lines (2 and 5) are about the same
block ...") is not in it.

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** An entry appears to be posted when its turn is finished (answered, or
superseded by the next sentence), so a page closed on an open question, a reload, or a throw before
the finish drops it. Posting at the ask and patching at the answer, or flushing on `pagehide`,
would keep them. The moved-then-refused case looks like the refusal path after a rerun not calling
the entry's finish.
