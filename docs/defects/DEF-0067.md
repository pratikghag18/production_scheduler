## Reproduction

Tester's stack, demo seed. As Dana give Sam Patel two blocks today 6 am to 2 pm: set his Cell 1 block to 0.5 and add
one on Cell 2 (0.5, a run on Cell 2). As **Ana**: `put Sam Patel and John Kim on Common Fastener at Cell 2 today from 8 am to 10 am`.
Read the question, press "Split evenly", read the listing. Restore the rows afterwards.

## Expected

R-468's question names where his time is split; the places should read as three distinct things or say that the new
block is on Cell 2 too, in words a supervisor would say.

## Actual

```
1 of 2: Sam Patel is already on Cell 1 today from 6 am to 2 pm and Cell 2 today from 6 am to 2 pm. Split Sam Patel's time evenly between Cell 1, Cell 2 and Cell 2, or skip Sam Patel?
... Sam Patel is on Cell 2 Thu Oct 1 from 8 am to 10 am, making Common Fastener, Sam Patel's time split evenly with Cell 1 and Cell 2.
```

The write itself is right (Cell 1 0.34, Cell 2 0.33, new Cell 2 block 0.33, read back as Dana).

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** The "between" list is the existing blocks' cells plus the new step's cell, with no
de-duplication. The listing's clause already de-duplicates ("with Cell 1 and Cell 2" for two Cell-2 blocks reads fine
there); the question could use the same list, or "between his two blocks and Cell 2".
