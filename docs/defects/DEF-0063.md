## Reproduction

Tester's stack, fresh demo seed. Sam Patel is on Cell 1 today 6 am to 2 pm; Priya Shah is on Cell 4 (Line 2, a
line Ana cannot read) today 6 am to 2 pm. As the plant admin, give Sam a SECOND block on Cell 1 today from 6 pm
to 8 pm (an `assignments` insert copying his first row with `timerange` `[2026-10-01 23:00+00, 2026-10-02
01:00+00)`; Priya has nothing then). Sign in as **Ana**, open the bar:

1. "replace Sam Patel with Priya Shah on Cell 1"
2. Read the sentence and the two buttons.
3. Press "Take Sam Patel off anyway".
4. Read the thread, then read Sam's and Priya's rows as Dana. Delete the two rows you added.

## Expected

R-466: the bar names why Priya cannot go and offers two choices, take the outgoing person off anyway (the question
says the place is left uncovered) or leave things as they are. The press does what the button says: it takes Sam
off. It does not write something the question never mentioned.

## Actual

Step 2, the sentence and buttons (identical to the one-block case):

```
Priya Shah is already on Cell 4 in Line 2 today from 6 am to 2 pm. Take Sam Patel off Cell 1 anyway? Nobody would be covering Sam Patel's hours on Cell 1.
buttons: ["Take Sam Patel off anyway","Leave it"]
```

Step 3, the thread after the press:

```
Done, 3 things. Written: Sam Patel is off Cell 1 Thu Oct 1; that was 6 am to 2 pm, making Housing A. Sam Patel is off Cell 1 Thu Oct 1; that was 6 pm to 8 pm, making Housing A. Priya Shah is on Cell 1 Thu Oct 1 from 6 pm to 8 pm, making Housing A.
```

Step 4, Priya's new row read back: `["2026-10-01 23:00:00+00","2026-10-02 01:00:00+00")` on Cell 1. The sentence said nobody would
be covering Sam's hours; for the 6 pm block that is false, and a block was written for a person the bar had just
said could not be placed. The after-the-fact readout does name it, but only after the press.

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** The probe's refusal is for the one busy block; "Take ... anyway" runs every kept step,
which are every removal plus every placement the probe let through. The developer's own note (F-248) reads it the
same way. Either ask once per block, or build the button's sentence from the steps it will run ("Take Sam Patel
off both blocks and put Priya Shah on the 6 pm one?"), or make the anyway press run only the removals.
No pin: the case needs the CommandBar harness the developer owns (CB-rep-2 with two Sam blocks, one of them
outside Priya's busy hours, is the shape).
