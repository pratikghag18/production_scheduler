operator rail; then run
`npx playwright test e2e/touch.spec.ts --workers=1` with the stack's env.

## Expected

any hour. R-448: the rail shows by default the people whose home shift covers now; a demo plant

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** R-441's hash homes Plant A's people on Shift 2 and 3 only; since R-448
the rail filters to the band covering now, so from 06:00 to 14:00 the demo rail is empty. The rail
code does say "No one on shift now" when no band covers now, but not when a band does and nobody is
homed on it. Either the seed spreads Plant A across all three shifts (a seed fix like R-452's), or
T3 presses the rail's shift chips first as `absenceOnBoard.spec.ts` already does, or both; and the
maintainer may want the rail to say "Nobody on Shift 1" rather than stand empty under a heading
that implies otherwise.
