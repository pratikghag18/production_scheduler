## Reproduction

Tester's stack, fresh demo seed. Priya Shah is on Cell 4 (Line 2) every day this week from 6 am to
2 pm. Sign in as **Ana** (`ana@example.test`, Line 1 supervisor), open the bar and type **"put Priya
Shah on Housing A at Cell 1 today from 10 am to noon"**, then press **Separate block**. Read Priya's
blocks back as the database owner.

Server half, no screen, `supabase-js` signed in as each person: `capacity_probe` for Priya over today
noon to 1 pm at 100% as Ana and as Dana (plant admin), then `create_assignment` for Priya as Ana on
Cell 2 (Line 1) noon to 1 pm, and as Dana on Cell 3 (Line 2) 1 pm to 1:30 pm. The same walk as one
command, with the stack's values exported: `node src/test/defects/DEF-0053.live.mjs` (it asks both
people, makes the booking as Ana, reads the load back, deletes its row; exit 1 while the defect
stands -- `DEFECT STANDS` on 30 Sept).

Pin (static, no stack): `npx vitest run src/test/defects/DEF-0053.test.ts` -- the last definition of
`operator_peak_load` in the migrations must run as a definer. If the fix takes another shape the
live script is the judge.

## Expected

R-033: "At any instant the sum of an operator's non-cancelled assignment efficiencies must not exceed
the org's cap (default 1.0). Split coverage such as 50/50 is legal; full double-booking is still
refused." The rule is about the person, not about who is asking. R-431: nothing the bar prints as
done is something the server's rule refuses.

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** `operator_peak_load` (last defined in migration 0043, read from the
live database with `pg_get_functiondef`) is `LANGUAGE sql STABLE` with no `SECURITY DEFINER`, and so
are `check_operator_capacity` (the `assignments_capacity` trigger) and `capacity_probe`
(`prosecdef = f` for all three). They sum `assignments` rows, and the `assignments_select` policy is
`node_id IN (SELECT app_readable_node_ids())`, so a supervisor's sum leaves out every block on a
place above or beside her grant. It is CLAUDE.md §4's rule about a node the caller cannot read,
on the oldest guard in the schema: the peak has to be computed by the server as a definer (guarding
the org boundary itself, as `absence_overlap` and `check_eligibility` already do -- both
`prosecdef = t`). `20_capacity_test.sql` runs as one role that reads everything, so it cannot see
this; a case with a scoped supervisor and a block outside her subtree would. The probe's
`overlapping` list is a separate question: a supervisor probably should be told "busy elsewhere"
without the row's details. The rail's "free" wording (R-438) is fed by the same read.
