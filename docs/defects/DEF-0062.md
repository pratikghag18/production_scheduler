## Reproduction

Tester's stack, fresh demo seed (Sam Patel on Cell 1, Line 1; Priya Shah on Cell 4, Line 2, every day
6 am to 2 pm, a line Ana cannot read). Sign in as **Ana**, open the bar:

1. **"replace Sam Patel with Priya Shah on Cell 1"** (today, during Priya's Cell 4 hours).
2. Read the thread and the buttons offered. Press the one offered.
3. Read Sam's and Priya's blocks back as Dana.

## Expected

R-466 (stated by the maintainer, 30 Sept): the bar says in one plain sentence why Priya cannot go
("Priya Shah is already on Cell 4 in Line 2 today from 6 am to 2 pm.") and ASKS what to do next, with
both choices as buttons: take Sam Patel off anyway (saying the cell is left uncovered), or leave things
as they are. Nothing is written before the answer. R-435 as restated the same day: "Whenever the bar is
in any doubt or instructions are unclear, it should always ask the user for what it should do next."

## Actual

Found in tester session 197t's adversarial pass (the report's words): the incoming half is dropped up
front and the bar offers only "Sam Patel is off Cell 1". One press removed Sam's Cell 1 block and left
the cell uncovered. The busy reason is disclosed in words, which is why it was first held as consistent
with R-432; the maintainer ruled that a replace is one intent and the bar must ask instead.

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** Session 196's lot pre-check (`refuseBusyLotSteps` in
`src/features/board/components/CommandBar.tsx`) treats the replace as a lot of two independent steps
(remove Sam, place Priya), refuses the busy step, and carries on with the rest as "Ready to do 1 thing".
A replace (and any lot whose remaining steps only make sense together) likely needs to turn a refused step
into a question rather than a shortened lot. The same question-instead-of-shrink rule would also answer
DEF-0061 (a lot that meets a certificate question).
