## Reproduction

Tester's stack, fresh demo seed (Sam Patel is on Cell 1 today 6 am to 2 pm; Cell 2 has nothing 8 am to 10 am; Lena
Novak lacks Welding, which Cell 2 needs). Sign in as **Ana**, open the bar:

1. "put Sam Patel on Housing A at Cell 2 today from 8 am to 10 am" (the single sentence, for comparison).
2. "put Sam Patel and Lena Novak on Housing A at Cell 2 today from 8 am to 10 am"; at "2 of 2: Not done: Lena Novak
   is not certified for Cell 2 ..." type "covering an absence"; then type "yes".
3. Read the thread and Sam's rows as Dana.

(The certificate question is only there to get a lot of two; the same happens with two certified people when one is
already booked on a place Ana can read at that time.)

## Expected

R-431: "Every button the bar offers and every readout it prints as done is decided by the check the server runs:
certificates, the area rule, capacity, absence. A refusal that arrives after the yes is a defect." The lot's
listing should have named Sam as not doable before asking for the yes, the way the single sentence is held back.

## Actual

Step 1: the bar says "Sam Patel is already booked then. The board is asking how to split the time. Answer it on the
board." (no write; the answer is held back before any yes).

Step 2, the listing, then the yes:

```
Ready to do 2 things: 1. Sam Patel is on Cell 2 Thu Oct 1 from 8 am to 10 am, making Housing A. 2. Lena Novak is on Cell 2 Thu Oct 1 from 8 am to 10 am, making Housing A. The reason given: covering an absence. Say yes to do them, or no.
yes
I made none of the 2 changes.
Not done: Sam Patel on Cell 2 Thu Oct 1, 8 am to 10 am. Sam Patel would be over the cap today (200% of 100%). Nothing changed.
Not tried: Lena Novak is not on Cell 2 Thu Oct 1, 8 am to 10 am.
```

Nothing is written (rows read back unchanged), and the refusal is honest, so this is minor; but it arrives after the
yes, and "Lena Novak is not on Cell 2" reads as though she was never going to be. The same with Lena already on Cell
1 at the same hours (200% of 100%, 13 Oct). Session 196's pre-check (`refuseBusyLotSteps`) only covers a block on a
line Ana cannot read; the readable overlap is left to the server.

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** `refuseBusyLotSteps` in `src/features/board/components/CommandBar.tsx` probes the
server's busy-elsewhere answer only for people the caller cannot read. A step whose person has a block the caller
CAN read at that time falls through to the writer, which the capacity trigger refuses. Either run the same probe for
every lot step, or treat the readable overlap as the single sentence does (the split question). Not caused by
session 198; found while attacking its new doors, and it sits next to F-247.

## Maintainer's decision -- 1 Oct, session 199t conversation

Of the two ways the Lead names, the maintainer chose the second ("I agree with option B."): the lot asks
the overlapping step's question the way the single sentence does, then carries on to the next step. It is
R-468 in the plan; this defect now violates it as well as R-431. Refusing the step up front (option A) was
the alternative and was not chosen.
