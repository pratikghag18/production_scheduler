## Reproduction

Tester's stack, fresh demo seed (Lena Novak lacks Welding, which Cell 2 needs; Sam Patel and Priya
Shah are certified). Sign in as **Ana**, open the bar, use a day with nothing on Cell 2 so nothing
else interferes (2026-10-13 was used). The parse of all three sentences is the same kind, a lot of two
(`parseCommand` answers `intent: several` with both people).

1. **"put Lena Novak and Sam Patel on Housing A at Cell 2 2026-10-13 from 3 pm to 5 pm"** -- the bar
   says "Not done: Lena Novak is not certified for Cell 2, missing Welding. Say the reason to schedule
   anyway, or no." Type **"covering an absence"**, Enter. Then read Sam's rows as Dana.
2. **"put Sam Patel and Lena Novak on Housing A at Cell 2 2026-10-14 from 3 pm to 5 pm"** (the other
   order) -- the bar says "2 of 2: Not done: Lena Novak is not certified for Cell 2, missing Welding. Say
   the reason to schedule anyway, or no." Type **"covering an absence"**, Enter, three times, waiting a few
   seconds between.

Also the same on the build BEFORE session 196 (f0f4a4b, checked on a throwaway worktree on its own dev
server): this is not from a6b1454.

## Expected

R-425: "a lot is refused before its yes, naming the person and the missing training" and "never writes
half of itself"; the question's own code says a lot takes no reason (`inLot` yields "Nothing
changed."). R-432: a sentence that becomes several changes names every change in two groups, what was
done and what was not, "and every change after it that was never tried". The bar must not ask for a
reason it will not take.

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** A lot's step that is short a certificate is asked about one step at a time
through the ordinary single-sentence question path, which sets `awaitingOverrideReason` for the
question's text but does not hold the rest of the lot (`lotRef`) across the answer: step 1's answer
ends the turn, dropping the remaining steps (sentence 1), and at step 2 the reason-awaiting flag is
not set for the lot's own step (sentence 2; `questionToStatus` only sets it when `!question.inLot`, while
the text builder in `resolve.ts` ("Say the reason to schedule anyway, or no.") does not check the same
flag the same way for this path). Per R-425 the intended answer is the refusal of the whole lot before
its yes ("... Nothing changed."), or the reason accepted for that step and the lot carried on. No
pin was written: the bug is in the bar's lot stepping across a question, which needs the CommandBar
harness the developer owns; the two sentences above are the reproduction.

## Developer note -- 30 Sept, session 198

Agreed, with one correction to the lead. The Sam-first order was the held command: a lot never sets
`heldRef`, so the reason branch found null and returned without a word. The Lena-first order was a
second bug in front of it: 2026-10-13 was off the board, so Lena's step asked day_off_board, the board
moved, and the rerun re-ran that one step as a single sentence, abandoning the lot (F-246). The
maintainer chose, from two candidates drawn side by side, that a lot of independent steps asks the
step's reason and carries on rather than refusing whole as R-425 read; that is R-467. Now: "1 of 2:
Not done: Lena Novak is not certified for Cell 2, missing Welding. Say the reason to schedule anyway,
or no." takes the reason for Lena's step only, Sam's step resolves, the listing names both with
Lena's reason, and the one yes writes both -- Lena's row with eligibility_override and the reason,
Sam's without. Under block Lena is named "Not doing ...: she is not certified ..." and Sam listed. The
lot writer also never sent a step's reason before, so the server would have refused the step just
answered for; it passes it now. Seen in the browser as Ana in both orders, rows read back and
removed; held by CB-lot-ask-1 to 10 and a typed-walk case (both orders).
