## Reproduction

Tester's stack, demo seed (Sam Patel on Cell 1 today 6 am to 2 pm; Lena Novak lacks Welding, which Cell 2 needs). As
**Ana**: `put Sam Patel and Lena Novak on Common Fastener at Cell 2 today from 8 am to 10 am`. Read each question as
it arrives; answer `covering an absence`, press "Split evenly", type `yes`. Restore the rows afterwards.

## Expected

R-468: the overlap question is "numbered as the lot's step"; a supervisor reads "1 of 2" before "2 of 2".

## Actual

```
STEP0: 2 of 2: Not done: Lena Novak is not certified for Cell 2, missing Welding. Say the reason to schedule anyway, or no.
STEP1: 1 of 2: Sam Patel is already on Cell 1 today from 6 am to 2 pm. Split Sam Patel's time evenly between Cell 1 and Cell 2, or skip Sam Patel?
STEP2: Ready to do 2 things: 1. Sam Patel is on Cell 2 Thu Oct 1 from 8 am to 10 am, making Common Fastener, Sam Patel's time split evenly with Cell 1. 2. Lena Novak is on Cell 2 Thu Oct 1 from 8 am to 10 am, making Common Fastener. The reason given: covering an absence. Say yes to do them, or no.
```

Both questions are right and the write is right (Sam Cell 1 0.5, Sam Cell 2 0.5, Lena Cell 2 1.0, read back); only
the order of the numbers reads backwards.

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** The certificate questions are asked at the first stage of the lot and the overlap
questions after the pre-check, so the lower number is asked last. Ask them in step order, or drop the "N of M" from
whichever stage comes second.

## Developer note -- 2 Oct, session 202

Agreed. The lot now resolves every step first (a step that needs only a reason is resolved with a
placeholder and its question queued), runs the one probe, and then asks every question in step order,
numbered over the steps left after the probe: "1 of 2: Sam Patel is already on Cell 1 ..." then "2 of 2:
Not done: Lena Novak is not certified ...". The same change closes F-247: a step both uncertified and
busy elsewhere is refused with no reason asked. Held by CB-ord-1 to 5 and a typed-walk case; no placeholder
reason can reach a write (the reviewer's scripted cases). Two edges noted in the plan: a step whose
question is of another kind (which shift, join) is still asked at once while stepping and so can come
before an earlier step's reason; and "k of N" counts the steps left after the probe.
