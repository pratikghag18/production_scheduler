## Reproduction

Tester's stack, demo seed. Sign in as **Ana** (Line 1 supervisor; her board shows Cell 1 and Cell 2).
Open the bar and type **"clear Cell 3 today"**.

## Expected

R-430 (a standard): "When a sentence names something the board cannot find (a person, a place, a
product, a day off the board), the bar offers the closest matches as buttons and asks which. 'No
such X' with no choice is a defect."

## Actual

```
SAY  clear Cell 3 today
BAR  No cell called "Cell 3" on this board.
```

No buttons. (The trace has the turn only because a later sentence closed it -- DEF-0049.)

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** The unknown-cell refusal seems to offer matches only when a name is
close to one on the board; "Cell 3" is exactly a cell that exists in the plant but not on Ana's
board, and "Cell 1"/"Cell 2" are one character away. A sentence the server would refuse anyway (Cell
3 is on Line 2, outside her grant) should say that in words -- "Cell 3 is on Line 2, which is not on
your board" -- and offer her own cells, rather than stop.
