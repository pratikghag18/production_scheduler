## Reproduction

Tester's stack, demo seed, as ana@example.test (Line 1 board, shift 1 on now; Priya is on Cell 4 today 6 am to 2 pm
Chicago, run at about 12:40 pm).

1. Open the board as Ana. The rail reads `Priya Shah ... booked (Cell 4, 6 a...`.
2. Give Ana a read-only grant on Cell 4: `docker exec supabase_db_production_scheduler_tester psql -U postgres -c "insert into public.profile_grants(profile_id,node_id,org_id,role) select 'a0000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000002', id, org_id, 'viewer' from public.nodes where name='Cell 4'"`. Reload the board.
3. The rail now reads `Priya Shah ... free`. (Asking the bar to put Priya on Cell 1 then says she is already on Cell 4 and Ana cannot change it.)
4. Remove the grant: `delete from public.profile_grants where profile_id='a0000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000002' and role='viewer'`.

## Expected

R-465: "The operator rail says the same of the same person ('booked', with the cell and the hours), for a line
supervisor exactly as for the plant admin." Giving a person more read access must not turn "booked" into "free".

## Actual

Screenshots taken (rail with and without the grant): with the grant `free`, without `booked (Cell 4, 6 a...`.

## Lead

**A suspicion, not a fact.** The rail is fed by the board's own window (rows under Ana's root, Line 1) plus
`operator_blocks_elsewhere`, which returns only blocks on places the caller CANNOT read. A block on a readable place
outside the board's root is in neither source. Possibly the same cause as the 201t roleWalk R-465 flake (a spec holding a viewer grant for Ana
while roleWalk reads her rail, under parallel workers); a hypothesis, not tested. A fix could make the set cover
every block of a readable person off the board's window, not only unreadable places.
